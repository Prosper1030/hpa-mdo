## Bottom Line

The current z-state cliff is being produced by the canonical dual-beam/inverse route, not the old Phase 9 scalar proxy. But the canonical route is still a production-mainline model with spot-check validation, not final structure-grade sizing. Therefore the cliff is diagnostic evidence that must be audited, not a hard structural rejection.
